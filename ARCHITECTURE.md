# meta-signal-persona — architecture

*Meta policy contract for the Persona engine manager.*

## Surface

`meta-signal-persona` is the privileged companion contract to
`signal-persona`. It carries the meta plane for `persona`; ordinary component
lifecycle traffic — readiness, health reports, presence announcements, stop
acknowledgements — stays in `signal-persona`.

## Direction

This repo is the second leg of the Persona contract pair. Every Persona
component has exactly two contracts: the ordinary `signal-<component>` and
the meta `meta-signal-<component>`. `meta-signal-persona` is the authority
surface the owner speaks to manage engines and the components inside them.

The channel carries five operations:

```text
Query                            Response
├─ Launch(EngineLaunch)          ├─ Launched(LaunchAcceptance)
│                                └─ LaunchRejected(LaunchRejection)
├─ Retire(EngineIdentifier)      ├─ Retired(EngineIdentifier)
│                                └─ RetireRejected(RetirementRejection)
├─ Start(ComponentStartup)       ├─ ActionAccepted(ActionAcceptance)
├─ Stop(ComponentShutdown)       └─ ActionRejected(ActionRejection)
└─ Query(MetaQuery)              ├─ Catalog(EngineCatalog)
   ├─ Catalog(EngineCatalogScope)├─ EngineStatus(EngineStatusReport)
   ├─ EngineStatus(…Scope)       ├─ ComponentStatus(ComponentStatus)
   └─ ComponentStatus(…Name)     └─ ComponentMissing(ComponentName)
```

Rejection is always typed: `LaunchRejectionReason`,
`RetirementRejectionReason` and `ActionRejectionReason` enumerate the closed
set of refusals. There is no free-text error.

## Where the quoted lifecycle types live

`ComponentName`, `ComponentDesiredState`, `ComponentStatus` and
`EngineIdentifier` are declared once, in `signal-persona`'s
`ethos/signal.ethos`, and imported here by name. `signal-persona` declares
`links = "signal-persona"`, so cargo admits exactly one revision of it in any
dependency graph: every contract in the estate must pin the same revision, or
resolution fails before compilation.

## Boundaries

This crate owns:

- the meta request and reply vocabulary for the Persona engine manager;
- engine-scoped records: `EngineLabel`, `EngineGeneration`, `EnginePhase`,
  `EngineCatalog`, `EngineStatusReport`;
- the typed acceptance and rejection records;
- the rkyv and Datom derives for the meta contract.

This crate does not own:

- the `persona` manager runtime or its scheduling policy;
- ordinary component lifecycle traffic;
- the component lifecycle vocabulary it imports.

## Invariants

- Every component has exactly two public contracts:
  `signal-<component>` and `meta-signal-<component>`.
- The contract is authored in ethos. `src/generated/signal.rs` is generated by
  `ethos-zero` and committed; `build.rs` fails the build when it drifts from a
  fresh generation. No hand-written `Datomic` impl for a declared type.
- Every LiGoldragon dependency is pinned by immutable `rev`, never by branch.
- Every head in the contract has a round-trip witness in `tests/contract.rs`
  and a line in `examples/canonical.datom`, and the canonical file is gated:
  every non-comment line must actualize into a `Query` or a `Response`.

## Code Map

```text
ethos/signal.ethos        the contract source
src/generated/signal.rs   ethos-zero output, committed, freshness-asserted
src/lib.rs                the rkyv Signal frame surface
examples/canonical.datom  canonical Datom text for every head
tests/contract.rs         round-trip and canonical-file witnesses
```
